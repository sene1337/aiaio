AIAIO — six ways to move through a session

concept mockups · everything decided stays (task race, wall of forgetting, token economy, subagents, your words as content) — only the traversal changes

▼THE SCROLLBACK

Fall down your own transcript. Text lines are platforms; the forgetting chases through the scrollback.

◈THE RAIL MAP

The session as a metro map of turns — tool calls are side-tracks, subagents are branch lines, the red front swallows stations.

◯THE RING BUFFER

The context window as a circle you orbit. A sweep-hand of forgetting chases you; compaction shrinks the world.

≡THE CALL STACK

Climb the session as a tower of stack frames while the corruption tide rises and floors pancake-collapse.

⇶THE THREE LANES

Time cannot stop. Hop between user/assistant/tool lanes as the session streams past — the left edge is the forgetting.

⌂THE MEMORY PALACE

Isometric rooms, one per chapter. Look back through a doorway and watch the room you just left dissolve.

Each mockup is a self-contained attract-mode animation with a DESIGN NOTES panel (what changes, what stays, why it might be more fun, top risks). Mocked with the chaotic example session's real task names and moments.
